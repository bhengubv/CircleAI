# Emergencies and staying safe

Save these now, while it's calm. In real danger, **call — don't wait.**

## The numbers

- **Police: 10111**
- **Ambulance: 10177**
- **From any cellphone: 112** — works even with **no airtime and a locked phone**, and
  routes you to the nearest help. If you remember only one number, remember this.
- **Abuse / gender‑based violence (women and children): 0800 428 428**, or SMS the word
  **HELP to 31531**. Free and confidential.
- **A child in danger or who needs to talk: Childline 116** (free).
- **Swallowed poison / overdose: 0861 555 777** (Poison Helpline).

## When you call

1. Say **WHERE you are first** — address, or the nearest landmark, shop, or road.
2. Say **WHAT happened** and **how many people are hurt.**
3. **Don't hang up** until they tell you to — they may guide you while help comes.

## If someone is being hurt at home

It is **not your fault**, and help is **free and confidential** — call **0800 428 428**
or SMS **HELP to 31531**. If you are in immediate danger, call **10111** or **112**.

## Keep to hand

Write these numbers where your family can see them, and teach the children **112**.

*Tell me what's happening and I'll tell you exactly who to call.*
