# Plan your money this month

A budget is just a plan for your money so it lasts the month. It works even when
money is tight or comes in irregularly.

## Four steps

1. **Write down what comes in.** Your pay, any grant, piece work, anything. If it
   is irregular, use a low month so you are not caught short.
2. **Write down what must go out.** Rent, electricity, transport, food, school
   fees, airtime, and any debt you must pay.
3. **Split spending into NEEDS and WANTS.** Needs keep you going (food, transport
   to work, rent). Wants are nice but can wait. When money is short, needs first.
4. **Pay the most important things first** — the ones that cost you your home,
   your job, or your children's school if you miss them.

## Save a little, on purpose

- Even a small amount put aside each month becomes an **emergency buffer** so one
  bad week does not send you to a loan shark.
- A **stokvel** is a good way to save with people you trust — agree the rules and
  keep a record.
- Save *before* you spend what is left, not after.

## Stay away from expensive money

- **Avoid the mashonisa (loan shark).** The interest is brutal and they can trap
  you for months. If you must borrow, use a registered credit provider.
- Be careful with **buy-now-pay-later and store credit** — the total you repay is
  much more than the price on the shelf.
