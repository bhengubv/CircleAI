# Staying active and healthy

You do not need a gym or money to get fitter. A body that moves every day and
eats simply is most of it — and it protects you from diabetes, high blood
pressure and heart disease, which are common and serious in South Africa.

## Move every day, free

- **Walking** is the best place to start — brisk, most days, building up to about
  30 minutes. Walk to the shop, get off the taxi a stop early, take the stairs.
- **A short home workout**, no equipment: squats (standing up from a chair),
  push-ups (against a wall to start), lunges, and holding a plank. A few rounds,
  a few times a week, builds real strength.
- **Play** — soccer, netball, dancing — counts, and it is easier to keep up
  because it is fun.

## Eat simply and well

- More **vegetables and beans**, less sugar and less salt.
- **Water instead of cooldrink** — sugary drinks are a big hidden cause of weight
  gain and diabetes.
- You do not need supplements or special food. Plain, home-cooked, balanced meals
  beat them. See *Feeding your family well*.

## Keep it going

- **Start small and regular** beats a big plan you drop in a week. Ten minutes
  today is better than an hour you skip.
- Pair it with something you already do, or a friend, so it sticks.
- Enough **sleep** and less alcohol do as much as exercise.

## A note of care

If you are older, pregnant, very overweight, or have a heart condition or
diabetes, check with your **clinic** before starting hard exercise, and build up
gently. Stop and get help for chest pain or dizziness.
