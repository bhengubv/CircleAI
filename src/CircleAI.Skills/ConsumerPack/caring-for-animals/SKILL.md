# Looking after your animals

An animal that is fed, watered, and protected from disease stays healthy and
costs you far less than a sick one. Basic care is cheap; some of it is free.

## The basics every animal needs

- **Clean water always**, and the right food for the animal. A thin, dull animal
  is usually hungry, wormed too seldom, or sick.
- **Shelter** from sun, cold and rain.
- **Space and handling** without cruelty — a calm animal is easier and safer.

## Health and free help

- The **SPCA** and some welfare clinics offer **free or low-cost** treatment,
  vaccination and sterilisation for people who cannot afford a private vet — find
  your nearest branch.
- **Rabies is deadly and is the law**: dogs and cats should be **vaccinated
  against rabies**, and it is often free at state campaigns. If a person is bitten
  by any animal, wash the wound with soap and water for several minutes and go to
  the **clinic immediately** — rabies is preventable right after a bite but fatal
  once it starts.
- **Deworm and treat for ticks and fleas** regularly — cheap, and it prevents
  bigger problems.

## Small livestock

- **Dip or treat cattle, goats and sheep for ticks**, and follow your area's
  **dipping** programme.
- Notify the state vet of sudden deaths or many sick animals — some diseases
  (like foot-and-mouth) must be reported by law.
- Vaccinate poultry against the common killers; ask an extension officer or co-op.

## Being a responsible owner

- **Sterilise** pets you are not breeding — it prevents unwanted litters and
  roaming.
- A dog that bites or roams is your responsibility in law. Keep it secure.
