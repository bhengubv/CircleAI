# Running your household

A home runs better when the money and the admin are under control. A little
system beats a pile of unopened envelopes.

## Bills and municipal accounts

- Know your **fixed monthly costs** — rent or bond, electricity, water, rates —
  and put them first in your budget. See *Plan your money this month*.
- If your **municipal bill looks wrong** (a sudden huge amount, an estimate),
  query it at the municipal office with your meter reading — do not just ignore
  it, as arrears grow and services get cut.
- If you truly cannot pay, ask the municipality about a **payment arrangement** or
  an **indigent / free basic services** registration for low-income households —
  many qualify for free basic water and electricity and never claim it.

## Prepaid and meters

- **Prepaid electricity** helps you control spend — buy in the amount you can
  afford and watch what uses the most (geyser, heater, kettle). See *Electricity
  and load shedding*.
- **Read your water and electricity meters** yourself now and then — it catches a
  leak or a billing error early. A running toilet or a dripping tap wastes real
  money.

## Deposits and agreements

- Keep every **receipt and agreement** — deposit paid, lease, appliance warranty —
  in one place (a folder or photos on your phone). You will need them.
- Getting a **rental deposit back** is easier with photos of the place when you
  moved in and out. See *Renting a home*.

## Keep the home running

- A simple **chores rota** shares the load so it does not all fall on one person.
- A small **emergency kit** — candles, matches, a torch, water, basic first aid —
  saves a bad night during load shedding or a storm.
