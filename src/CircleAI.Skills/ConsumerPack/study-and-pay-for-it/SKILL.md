# Studying and paying for it

Money is the most common reason people stop studying, and there is more help than
most people know. Start early — funding has deadlines that close months before
classes begin.

## Ways to pay

- **NSFAS** funds students from poorer households at public universities and TVET
  colleges — tuition and often an allowance. Apply online at **nsfas.org.za**.
  Applications open and close on set dates each year, so check and apply the
  moment they open.
- **Bursaries** come from government departments, companies, and municipalities —
  often for scarce skills (teaching, nursing, engineering, accounting). Search
  and apply to several; many go unclaimed.
- **Learnerships** pay you a small allowance while you learn a trade and earn a
  qualification. Ask at your nearest **labour centre** or SETA.

## Paths that are not university

- **TVET colleges** teach practical trades (electrical, plumbing, hospitality,
  business) and are far cheaper. NSFAS funds these too.
- A **learnership or apprenticeship** earns while you learn.
- If you did not pass matric, you can **rewrite subjects** or study through adult
  education — ask at a TVET college or the Department of Basic Education.

## Applying well

1. Get your documents ready: **ID, matric results (or latest report), proof of
   household income**.
2. Apply to **several** places, not one — funding is competitive.
3. Keep copies and note every **deadline**. A missed deadline is the most common
   reason a good student gets nothing.

## Watch out

Nobody legitimate charges you a fee to "guarantee" a bursary or NSFAS. NSFAS
applications are **free** at nsfas.org.za.
