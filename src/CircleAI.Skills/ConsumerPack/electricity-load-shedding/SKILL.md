# Electricity and load shedding

## Prepaid and the schedule

- Buy prepaid tokens at shops, tills, or your banking app; keep a small buffer of
  **units** so you don't run out at night. Your meter shows how many are left.
- Know your area's **load‑shedding schedule** (an app like EskomSePush, or your
  municipality). Before it starts: **charge your phone and a light**, and fill a
  flask/bottles of water.

## Staying safe — this part matters most

Every winter people die from getting this wrong:
- **Never bring a petrol/diesel generator, a brazier (imbawula), or a braai indoors.**
  They give off **carbon monoxide** — a poison gas you cannot smell that kills you in
  your sleep. Run a generator **outside only**, away from windows and doors.
- **Candles and paraffin** cause fires and burns. Never leave them near curtains or
  bedding, never leave them burning unattended, and keep them away from children.
- **Switch off and unplug** the stove, iron and heater before the power goes, so they
  don't start a fire or surge when it comes back.
- If someone near a fire, stove, or generator becomes **dizzy, sleepy or has a
  headache**, get them into **fresh air** and call for help (**10177** or **112**).

## Save power (and money)

- Boil only the **water you need** in the kettle.
- Geyser on a **timer**, or switch it off when you're out — it's the biggest user.
- **Lids on pots**, LED bulbs, and switch off at the wall what you're not using.
