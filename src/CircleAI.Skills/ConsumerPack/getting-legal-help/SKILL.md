# Getting legal help for free

You do not need money for a lawyer to get help. This is general information, not
legal advice for your specific case.

## Legal Aid South Africa — free lawyers

- Free legal help **if you pass a means test** — roughly, if you earn under a set
  amount each month and don't have savings to pay a lawyer.
- **Call the free Advice Line: 0800 110 110** (weekdays, 7am–7pm). Can't call? Send a
  **"Please Call Me" to 079 835 7179** and they call you back.
- Or go to your nearest **Justice Centre** with your **ID, proof of income, and your
  case papers.**
- Legal Aid mostly helps with criminal cases and serious civil matters.

## Small Claims Court — for money owed (up to R20,000)

- Use it when someone **owes you money up to R20,000** — an unpaid loan, a deposit
  not returned, goods that were faulty.
- It is **free** (just a small fee for the summons and sheriff).
- You do **not** use a lawyer there — you tell the court yourself. A lawyer or the
  clerk can help you get ready.

## Other free help

- **University law clinics** and **advice offices** in many towns.
- **ProBono.Org** connects poor clients with lawyers who help for free.
- **Work problem** (unfair dismissal, unpaid wages)? That goes to the **CCMA** — free
  — not a normal court. Ask me about your rights at work.
