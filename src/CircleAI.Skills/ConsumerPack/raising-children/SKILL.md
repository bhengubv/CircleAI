# Raising children

Every child has the right to be registered, to be healthy, and to go to school —
and most of what they need from the state is **free**. Here is how to claim it and
raise them well on a tight budget.

## The first things to sort out

1. **Register the birth** at **Home Affairs** — free, and best done within **30
   days** of birth. The birth certificate is what unlocks the grant, the clinic
   and school.
2. **Apply for the Child Support Grant** at **SASSA** if you care for the child
   and your income is low. Take the child's birth certificate, your ID, and proof
   of income. See *Apply for a social grant*.
3. **Immunise at the clinic** — free, on the schedule in the child's Road-to-
   Health card. It protects them from diseases that still kill children. Keep the
   card safe.

## Health and feeding on a budget

- **Breastfeeding** is free and best for a baby's first months where possible;
  the clinic advises.
- Cheap, filling, healthy: maize meal with beans or eggs, spinach and other
  greens, seasonal vegetables, peanut butter. See *Feeding your family well*.
- Clean water, hand-washing, and the clinic for diarrhoea or fever — these save
  small children.

## School

- School is compulsory from age 7 (Grade 1). A **no-fee school** charges nothing,
  and you can apply for a **fee exemption** at a fee-charging school if you cannot
  afford it — a school may not refuse a child for unpaid fees.
- Register early for the next year; popular schools fill up.

## Discipline and safety

- Children learn from calm, firm, consistent limits, not from being beaten —
  praise what you want to see more of.
- Teach them **112** and who to go to if they are lost or scared.
- A child in danger or who needs to talk: **Childline 116** (free).
