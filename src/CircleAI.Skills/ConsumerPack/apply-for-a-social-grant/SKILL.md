# Apply for a social grant (SASSA)

**SASSA** (the South African Social Security Agency) pays grants to people who
qualify. Applying is **free** — never pay anyone who says they can get you a
grant for a fee.

## The main grants

- **Older Person's Grant** — for people 60 and older.
- **Disability Grant** — for adults who cannot work because of a disability.
- **Child Support Grant** — for the person caring for a child.
- **Foster Child Grant** — for a court-placed foster child.
- **Care Dependency Grant** — for a child with a severe disability needing
  full-time care.
- **Grant-in-Aid** — extra help if you already get a grant and need full-time
  care.
- **SRD grant** — temporary help for unemployed adults with no income.
- **War Veterans Grant.**

Grant **amounts change every year (usually in April)** — check the current amount
at **sassa.gov.za** or your nearest SASSA office.

## How to apply

- For most grants, go **in person to your nearest SASSA office**. If you cannot
  go yourself, someone can apply on your behalf with a doctor's note or power of
  attorney.
- The **SRD grant is applied for online** at **srd.sassa.gov.za** — you do not
  need to visit an office.

## What to bring

- Your **green ID book or smart ID card** (certified copy). No ID? SASSA can
  still help — ask; an affidavit and a temporary ID process exist.
- **Proof of where you live** and, for some grants, your **bank statements**.
- A **sworn affidavit** about your income (stamped by the police or a
  commissioner of oaths).
- For a disability or care grant, SASSA sends you for a **medical assessment** —
  bring all your medical records.
- For a child grant, the **child's birth certificate**.

## After you apply

You get a **receipt** — keep it. If your application is turned down you have the
right to **appeal**.

*Tell me who the grant is for and your situation, and I will help you work out
which grant fits and what to take with you.*
