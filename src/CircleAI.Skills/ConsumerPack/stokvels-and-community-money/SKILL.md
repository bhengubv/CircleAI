# Stokvels and community money

A stokvel is people who trust each other pooling money for a shared goal —
saving, groceries, a payout that rotates, or a burial society that helps when
someone dies. It is one of the strongest money tools in South Africa when it is
run cleanly.

## Kinds of stokvel

- **Savings / rotating** — everyone pays in the same amount each month; the pot
  goes to one member in turn, or is saved and split (often in December).
- **Grocery scheme** — you save through the year and buy food in bulk at year end.
- **Burial society** — members contribute so that when a member or their family
  dies, the society helps with funeral costs.

## Run it so it lasts

1. **Write the rules down** and have everyone agree and sign: the amount, the
   payment date, who gets paid when, what happens if someone misses, and how
   someone leaves.
2. **Two or three people** hold the money and the record, not one — and every
   member can see the book.
3. **Bank the money** in a group account rather than keeping cash with one person.
   Many banks have stokvel accounts.
4. **Record every payment in and out**, and read it out at each meeting.

## Staying safe

- Only join a stokvel of people you **know and trust**. Trust is the whole
  security — there is no CCMA for a stokvel.
- Be very careful of any "stokvel" or scheme promising to **double your money**
  or pay you for **recruiting others** — that is a pyramid scheme and it collapses,
  taking everyone's money. A real stokvel only ever pays out what members put in.

*Tell me what you want the stokvel for and how many people, and I will help you
write clear rules everyone can agree to.*
