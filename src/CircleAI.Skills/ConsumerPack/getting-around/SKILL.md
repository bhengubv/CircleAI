# Getting around

Most of South Africa moves by **minibus taxi, bus and train**. A little planning
saves money and keeps you safe.

## Planning a trip

1. **Work out your route** — which taxi rank or station, which line, and where you
   change. Ask locals or the rank marshal; routes are known by destination, not
   numbers.
2. **Check the cost** and carry the fare in **small notes and coins** — taxis
   often cannot change big notes, and you pass the fare up during the ride.
3. **Leave time** — taxis fill before they leave, and peak hours are busy. For a
   fixed appointment, travel earlier than you think.
4. For longer trips, **intercity buses** (and where they run, trains) are cheaper
   than driving — book ahead for the big lines.

## Staying safe on the move

- Keep your **phone and money out of sight**, especially at ranks and stations.
- Carry a **small bag in front of you**, not a backpack behind.
- Tell someone your **route and expected arrival**, and share your live location
  if you can.
- Trust your gut — if a vehicle or driver feels wrong, wait for the next one.
- Late at night, arrange to be **met** at the other end.

## If you drive

- Keep your **licence, and the car's licence disc, valid** — a fine for an
  expired disc is easy to avoid.
- Never drink and drive — the limit is low and roadblocks are common.
- Keep doors locked and windows up at quiet intersections.
