# If you are dismissed unfairly

If you were dismissed and you think it was unfair, you can take it to the
**CCMA** (the Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration). It is
**free** and you **do not need a lawyer**.

## The 30-day deadline — this is the important one

You must refer the dispute to the CCMA **within 30 days of being dismissed**.
That count includes weekends and public holidays. If you are late you have to ask
for permission (condonation) and it may be refused, so act quickly.

## How to refer it

1. Get the **LRA 7.11 referral form** — from any CCMA office, the Department of
   Employment and Labour, or the CCMA website (ccma.org.za).
2. Fill it in and **give a copy to your employer** — by hand, email, fax or
   registered post. Keep proof that you sent it.
3. Send or take the form to the CCMA.
4. The CCMA sets a date for **conciliation** (a meeting to try to settle). If
   that does not settle it, it goes to **arbitration**, where a commissioner
   decides.

## What counts as unfair

Broadly, a dismissal must have a **fair reason** (conduct, poor performance, or
the employer's operational needs) **and** a **fair process** (you were told the
problem and given a chance to respond). Being dismissed with no reason, or with
no hearing, is often unfair. Retrenchment has its own rules.

## Help yourself

- Keep your **contract, payslips, warnings and messages** — they are your
  evidence.
- Write down what happened and the dates while it is fresh.
- The CCMA can order your job back or compensation if the dismissal was unfair.
