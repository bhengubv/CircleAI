# Growing food at home

A small garden feeds a family and saves money. Start small, near water, where the sun
reaches most of the day.

## Easy, cheap growers

- **Spinach / Swiss chard (morogo), cabbage, onions, carrots, beans, pumpkin,
  butternut, tomatoes, potatoes.** These are forgiving and feed you well.
- Make **compost** from kitchen scraps and dry leaves — free food for the soil.
- **Water early morning or evening** so less is lost to the sun.

## When to plant

- Most of South Africa has **summer rain** — plant with the **first good rains in
  spring, about October to December.** Maize (mielies), beans and pumpkin go in then.
- The **Western Cape has winter rain** — plant in **autumn, about March to May.**

## Maize (mielies)

- Plant once the rains are steady. Keep the **weeds down early**, while the plants are
  small. It needs **sun and water while the cobs are filling.**

## A few chickens

- Give them **shelter** from sun, rain and predators, **clean water every day**, and
  kitchen scraps plus a little feed. They give **eggs** and eat pests.

## When to get an expert

For a **sick animal**, a crop disease, or which chemical to use, ask your local
**agricultural extension officer or co‑op** — don't guess with poisons or medicines.
