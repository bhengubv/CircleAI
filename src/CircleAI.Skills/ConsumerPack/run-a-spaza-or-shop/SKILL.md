# Run a spaza or small shop

A shop lives or dies on two numbers: what you **pay** for stock and what you
**sell** it for. Keep the gap healthy and control the cash, and it works.

## Pricing and margin

- Know the **cost of every item** including transport to fetch it. Your selling
  price must cover that cost **and** a margin, or you are working for nothing.
- Fast movers (bread, airtime, cooldrink) can carry a small margin because they
  sell often; slow movers need a bigger one.
- Round to prices people can pay with the coins they carry.

## Stock and deliveries

- **Buy your fast movers in bulk** from a wholesaler or cash-and-carry — the
  price per unit drops. Plan one bigger trip instead of many small ones to save
  transport.
- **Do not over-order** slow or perishable stock — money stuck on a shelf, or
  thrown away, is money lost.
- Keep a **short reorder list** on the wall: when an item hits its last few, it
  goes on the list.
- If a supplier delivers, **count and check every delivery against the invoice
  before you pay** — short deliveries are common.

## Control the cash

- Keep a **cash tin or float separate** from your own money. Bank takings
  regularly rather than keeping large cash on site — it is safer.
- Write down daily takings. See *Keep simple books*.
- Watch for the slow leak: giving credit ("I'll pay Friday") that never comes
  back. Decide your rule and stick to it.

## The rules

- A shop selling food usually needs a **certificate / permit from your
  municipality** and must keep food clean and cold. Ask your local municipal
  office.
- Buy from **legitimate suppliers** and keep invoices — it protects you and your
  customers.
