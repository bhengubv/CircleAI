# Buying and selling safely

Whether it is one item or a side trade, two things decide if it goes well: a
**fair price** and a **safe deal**.

## Pricing what you sell

- Look at what the **same item, same condition** is selling for near you. Price
  in that range.
- Be honest about condition — "screen has a scratch" sells; a hidden fault comes
  back.
- A clear photo in good light and a plain description sell faster than a low
  price.

## Meeting safely

- Meet in a **busy public place** in daylight — a mall, a garage forecourt, a
  police station parking area. Never alone at home or theirs.
- **Take someone with you** and tell another person where you are going.
- **Count the cash before you hand over the item.** For big amounts, meet inside
  a bank.

## Spotting a scam

- **"I'll pay extra, just refund the difference"** — a scam. So is a buyer who
  "overpays" and asks for change.
- A **payment SMS or proof-of-payment screenshot is not money.** Only release the
  item once the cash is in your hand or the money truly reflects in your account
  — a real EFT can take time, and screenshots are faked.
- **No deposit** to a stranger for goods you have not seen.
- If a deal feels rushed or too good, walk away. There is always another deal.

## If you are cheated

Keep the chat, the numbers, and any proof, and report it at a police station
(open a case) and to the platform you used.
