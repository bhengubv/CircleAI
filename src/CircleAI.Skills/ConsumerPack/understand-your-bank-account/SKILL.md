# Understand your bank account

A bank account keeps your money safer than cash and lets you get paid, buy
airtime, and send money. Here is what to understand.

## Choosing and reading it

- Many banks have a **low-cost or zero-monthly-fee account** for everyday use.
  Ask for the cheapest account that does what you need.
- Your **statement** lists every payment in and out. Read it each month and check
  for anything you do not recognise.
- Watch the **fees**: monthly account fee, cash withdrawal at an ATM, sending an
  EFT, and drawing cash at a till (often cheaper than an ATM). Small fees add up.

## Keep your money safe — this is the part scammers target

- **The bank will NEVER phone, SMS or email you asking for your PIN, your card
  number, or an OTP.** Anyone who does is a scammer. Do not share them, ever.
- An **OTP** (one-time PIN) is the key to your money. If someone asks you to read
  one out or forward it, stop — they are trying to take your money.
- Do not click links in SMS or WhatsApp that claim to be your bank. Open the
  bank's own app or type the website yourself.
- Cover your hand at the ATM. If a stranger "helps" you, walk away.

## If something goes wrong

- Phone your bank's **fraud line** immediately (the number is on the back of your
  card and on their website) and ask them to block the card.
- Report it and keep the reference number.
- You can open a case with the bank's ombud if the bank will not help.
