# Start and run a small business

You do not need money or a company to start. Most small businesses in South
Africa begin **informally** — one person, one skill, a phone. Register later,
when it helps you.

## Start small and legal

1. **Pick one thing you do well** and one customer who will pay for it. Test it
   cheaply before you spend.
2. **You may trade informally** as a sole trader — no registration needed to
   start. Keep it clean: a separate cash tin or a second bank account so your
   business money and your own money never mix.
3. **Price to cover everything** — materials, transport, airtime, your time — and
   still leave a profit. Selling below cost is the fastest way to close.

## When to register

- **CIPC** registers a company. It is cheap and can be done online at
  **bizportal.gov.za**. Register when a customer, a tender, or a supplier asks
  for a registered business, or when you want to protect the name.
- **SARS**: once you earn above the tax threshold you must register and file.
  Even below it, keeping records makes this painless later. See *Keep simple
  books*.
- Some trades need a **permit or licence** from your municipality (food, liquor,
  a physical shop). Ask your local municipal office first.

## Keep it going

- Put a little profit back in every month before you take money out.
- Serve the customer you have well — word of mouth is free advertising. See
  *Find and keep customers*.
- Watch your cash weekly, not once a year.

## Avoid the traps

- **A registered company you never file for** grows penalties. Only register when
  you are ready to keep it up, or use bizportal's guidance.
- Anyone charging you a big fee to "register your business fast" — you can do it
  yourself on bizportal.gov.za for a small official fee.
