# Find and keep customers

The cheapest customer is the one you already have coming back, and the next
cheapest is the one they tell about you. You do not need to pay for advertising
to start.

## Find the first ones

- **Tell everyone you know** what you do, in one clear sentence. Ask them to pass
  it on.
- **A WhatsApp status or Business profile** is free: a photo of your work, your
  price, your area, your number. Post when you have something to show.
- **Stand where your customers are** — near the taxi rank, the school, the shops
  — with a clear sign or a flyer.
- Ask a happy customer for the **names of two people** who might also want it.

## Keep them coming back

- **Do what you said, when you said, at the price you said.** That is most of it.
- **Save their number** and follow up: "Your order is ready", "It's been a
  month, need more?"
- A small thank-you or a regular's discount costs little and is remembered.
- **Fix a complaint fast and without argument.** A problem handled well makes a
  customer more loyal, not less.

## Keep track simply

A list — name, number, what they bought, when — is enough. It tells you who to
call and who has gone quiet. See *Keep simple books*.

## Avoid

- Promising what you cannot deliver to win a sale — it loses you the next ten.
- Spending on printed adverts or "boosted posts" before free word of mouth is
  working.
