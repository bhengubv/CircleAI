# Getting out of debt

If your debt repayments and living costs add up to **more than you earn**, you
are what the law calls **over-indebted**. South Africa's **National Credit Act**
gives you a way out called **debt review** (also called debt counselling).

## What debt review does

- A **registered debt counsellor** looks at what you earn, what you must live on,
  and what you owe.
- If you are over-indebted, they negotiate with the people you owe to **lower
  your monthly payments** (often lower interest and a longer time to pay).
- Everything you owe is combined into **one affordable payment** each month.
- The arrangement is confirmed by a **court or the National Consumer Tribunal**.

## The protection you get

- While you keep paying, creditors **cannot take legal action or repossess** your
  house or car for the debts under review.
- Collection calls should go to your counsellor, not to you.

## Be careful

- Only use a counsellor **registered with the National Credit Regulator (NCR)** —
  you can check on the NCR website. There are scams.
- Debt review is **not** the same as being "blacklisted". It is a legal process
  to help you pay, and it ends when your debts are cleared.
- You usually cannot take out new credit while under debt review — that is part
  of how it protects you.

## Where to get help

The **National Credit Regulator (NCR)** oversees debt counsellors and can point
you to a registered one. Do not pay large up-front fees to anyone promising to
"wipe" your debt.
