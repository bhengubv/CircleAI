# Feeding people for a living

Selling food — a kota stand, plates at the rank, catering, a small kitchen — can
be a good living. It stands on three things: **safe food, a fair price, and the
right permit.**

## Keep the food safe

Making people sick will close you faster than anything.

- **Wash hands** often; keep the cooking area, boards and knives clean.
- **Keep hot food hot and cold food cold.** The danger zone is lukewarm food left
  out — that is where bugs grow.
- **Cook meat and chicken right through.** Do not let raw meat touch ready food.
- Use **clean, safe water**, and cover food against flies and dust.
- If you are ill (especially stomach illness), do not handle food.

## The permit

- A business handling food for the public usually needs a **Certificate of
  Acceptability** from your **municipal / environmental health office** — it is
  about a clean, safe place to prepare food. Ask them what your setup needs before
  you grow.
- Selling **liquor** needs a separate **liquor licence** — trading without one is
  a serious offence.

## Price a plate to profit

- Add up the **cost of everything in one plate** — ingredients, gas, packaging —
  then price so that covers the cost **and** leaves a profit. Do not guess.
- Know your **best sellers** and make more of those; cut what does not sell before
  it spoils.
- Buy staples in **bulk** from a cash-and-carry to lower cost per plate.

## Grow it

- **Consistency** brings people back — same taste, same portion, same time open.
- A clean stall and a friendly face are free advertising. See *Find and keep
  customers*.
