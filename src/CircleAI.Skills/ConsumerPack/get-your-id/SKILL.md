# Get your ID and documents (Home Affairs)

Your ID unlocks almost everything — a job, a bank account, a grant, a SIM. Here's
how to get it from **Home Affairs (DHA)**. Requirements can change, so confirm at
the office or at **ehome.dha.gov.za**.

## Your first smart ID

- You can get it from **age 16**, and your **first smart ID is free.**
- **Where:** a Home Affairs office, **or** a bank branch that does IDs — Absa, FNB,
  Nedbank, Standard Bank, Capitec. The bank branches are often **faster**.
- **Bring:** your **birth certificate**, **proof of address**, and a completed
  **Form BI‑9** (black ink, capital letters — you can get it at the office).
- Someone **under 18** must have a **parent or guardian there** with them.
- It takes about **14 working days.**

## No birth certificate?

If your birth was never registered, you must **register it at Home Affairs first**
(late registration of birth), then apply for your ID. Bring whatever proof you have
of when and where you were born, and people who can confirm it.

## Registering a new baby

Register a baby's birth **within 30 days** at Home Affairs — it's **free.** Bring
both parents' IDs and the **proof of birth** from the clinic or hospital.

## Tip

Go **early in the morning** — the queues are long. Take all your documents plus a
copy of each.
