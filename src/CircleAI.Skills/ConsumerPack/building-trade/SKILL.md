# Working in the building trade

Good building work sells itself, but the money is won or lost on the **quote** and
on **getting paid**. Get those right and the work keeps coming.

## Quoting a job

1. **See the job in person** before you price it. Photos hide surprises.
2. Price **materials + labour + transport + a margin**. Add a bit for the things
   you cannot see behind a wall.
3. Give the quote **in writing** — what is included, what is not, and how long it
   is valid. A written quote prevents "but you said…".
4. Do not let a customer talk you below cost. Walking away beats losing money.

## Getting paid

- **Take a deposit** for materials before you start — you should not fund the
  customer's job out of your pocket.
- For a bigger job, agree **payment stages** (deposit, halfway, on completion) in
  writing.
- Keep it in writing over WhatsApp if nothing else — a record is what you have if
  there is a dispute. For unpaid work you can use the **Small Claims Court** for
  free. See *Getting legal help for free*.

## Materials and quality

- Buy the **right materials for the job** — the wrong cement, mix or wiring fails
  and your name is on it.
- Do not cut corners on things that are hidden but matter (foundations, damp,
  wiring). Callbacks cost more than doing it once.

## Safety — this is not optional

- **Electrical and gas work** must be done by a **registered, certified person** —
  a wrong connection kills, and a Certificate of Compliance is required by law.
- Use a hard hat, watch for falls from height, brace trenches, and keep the site
  safe for the people who live there.
