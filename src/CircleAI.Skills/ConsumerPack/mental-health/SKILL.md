# Looking after your mental health

Struggling in your mind is as real as any other illness, it is common, and it can
get better. You do not have to pay, and you do not have to face it alone.

## If you are in crisis right now

If you are thinking of ending your life, or you are with someone who is, please
reach out this minute — it is free, 24 hours:

- **SADAG Suicide Crisis Line: 0800 567 567** (24 hours)
- **SMS 31393** and a counsellor will call you back
- In immediate danger: **police 10111** or, from any cell, **112**

You are not a burden, and this feeling can pass with help.

## Free helplines that will just talk to you

- **SADAG Mental Health Line: 011 234 4837**
- **Adcock Ingram Depression & Anxiety Helpline: 0800 70 80 90**
- **Childline (for anyone under 18): 116** (free)

These are staffed by people trained to listen, in confidence. Calling is a
strong thing to do, not a weak one.

## Things that help day to day

- **Tell one person you trust** how you are really doing. Saying it out loud
  lightens it.
- **Small routine** — getting up, washing, a short walk, sleep at regular times —
  steadies the mind even when motivation is gone.
- **Move a little and go outside** if you can; it genuinely lifts mood.
- **Go easy on alcohol and drugs** — they make anxiety and depression worse after
  the short relief.
- Your **clinic** can help too, and can refer you to a counsellor or doctor for
  free.

## When to get help now

Reach out today if the low feeling has lasted weeks, if you cannot sleep or eat,
if you cannot cope with daily life, or if you have any thought of harming
yourself. This is what the numbers above are for.
