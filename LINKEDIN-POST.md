Circle AI is a place for your AI to live.

On your phone. On your laptop. On the server in the rack you control. MIT-licensed. Free. On the public NuGet registry as of this morning.

It's not a chat interface. It's not a model. It's the layer underneath — the thing that loads a model onto your device and lets your apps reach for it without renting that reach from anyone.
